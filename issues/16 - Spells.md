Spell ideas:
Equivalent Theft - an item in your hands is swapped with a different item of your choice within some range, both items have to have the same of one of the following attributes: value, material, item type, weight/size. There might be a check involved, giving increasing accuracy: random item in the vicinity, random item from the target, the desired item

Ice Beam - i don’t know if we’re doing damage types, but a cold damage ray/line with a chance to freeze on crits?

Dazzling Gleam - AoE blinding effect, maybe it deals super-effective damage against dragons

Scald - a spray of superheated water, might have a secondary metal-heating effect, boils soup

Metronome - causes a random spell rolled from a table to be cast, useless spells like splash are more common, but could be really stronk

Trick Room - fast things go slow, slow things go fast

Thesean recovery - a damaged or destroyed item is replaced piece-by-piece over the course of your turn until a pristine copy of the original item is in its place. But is it the same item as you started with?

Mutual Slumber - Choose a target and roll spellcasting, both you and the target fall asleep, you can choose any duration up to an amount determined by your roll: 1 round, 1 Tick, 1 day, etc. If either sleeper is woken by a 1AP action, both awake

Soil instability - turn the ground in an area into a semi-liquid, trapping creatures inside the area and making it difficult terrain.

Telekinesis - move an item no bigger than a golden horse statue 5 squares per turn

Temporary mutation - Roll spellcasting (maybe with some penalty), this determines the tier of mutation you bestow to a target

Curse of Zeno - space shifts around the target such that when they move they spend their entire movement moving towards their intended destination, but end up only halfway to their intended destination from where they started

Exhume/inhume - a target you are aware of within a range is either buried in the ground, or rises to the surface

Kaita/Nui (probably better to call this Fuse or something) - roll a spell check, the amount of targets is based on the roll: 2, 3, 6, all within range. All creatures targeted become merged into one greater creature, take the greatest attribute from all the component creatures to determine the new creature’s attributes, it also gains access to all the abilities of the component creatures

Probability shroud - the target can only be hit on a 3-in-6 (or other 50/50) whenever any ability would target them (this is a bad idea)

Expand - the target is expanded, the item takes up more space, the creature is larger on the battlefield, if cast on the mana gateway it expand
