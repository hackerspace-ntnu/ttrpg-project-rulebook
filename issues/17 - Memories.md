During character creation, players have to answer some questions about their character that grounds them in the world. The answers must be memories of something that happened to or because of their character.

- The first time a character Corrupt any Focus, they must cross out one memory.
- When they've Corrupted half of their Focus, they lose another memory.
- When they Corrupt their last Focus, they lose their core memory.
    - Alternatively give them the question "what is left?" or "what makes you human still?", and decide in the moment what they lose?
